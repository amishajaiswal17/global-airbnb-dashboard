## GLOBAL AIRBNB PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD | POWER BI ##
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Airbnb's global listings, host trust, pricing, ratings, and review behavior across 10 major cities — built to uncover growth patterns, market concentration, and guest trust signals from raw listings and reviews data.

📌 Project Overview

This project transforms raw Airbnb listings and reviews data into a 3-page interactive Power BI report that helps stakeholders understand where Airbnb is growing, which cities dominate the market, how guests rate their stays, and how trustworthy the platform's hosts are. The dashboard is designed to answer business questions a real Airbnb analytics or strategy team would ask — not just display charts.

❓ Problem Statement

Airbnb operates across dozens of cities with vastly different growth stages, pricing tiers, and guest experiences. Without a consolidated view, it's difficult to answer questions such as:


Which cities drive the majority of listings and revenue, and is the market concentrated or evenly spread?
How has Airbnb's growth evolved over time, and what external events (e.g., COVID-19, regulation) shaped it?
Are hosts trustworthy, and how does identity verification correlate with guest confidence?
Which cities/property types deliver the best (and worst) guest experience, and on which specific dimensions?
How frequently do guests leave reviews, and are there seasonal booking patterns by region?


This dashboard consolidates listings and reviews data into one report to answer these questions at a glance.

🧩 Dashboard Structure (3 Pages)

1️⃣ Overview


KPI summary cards: 279,712 listings, 182,024 hosts, 10 cities, 144 property types, 5.37M reviews
A lifecycle/growth area chart (2008–2021) segmented into Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline → Reinvention → COVID-19 phases, broken down by listing type (Hotel Room, Entire Place, Private Room, Shared Room)
Narrative insights on Airbnb's peak growth year (2015), the impact of tightening local regulations (2016–17), profitability milestones, and the COVID-19 disruption from 2019 onward.

2️⃣ Ratings


Market Share by City — a cumulative % chart showing Paris, New York, and Sydney alone account for ~48% of total listings/reviews, with superhost vs. non-superhost listing split
Average price comparison across room types (Hotel Room, Entire Place, Shared Room, Private Room)
Ratings matrix across 10 cities on 5 sub-metrics: Accuracy, Cleanliness, Communication, Location, Value — with conditional formatting to instantly flag low scorers
A toggle-able Overall vs. Detailed Rating view, identifying Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro as top-rated cities, and Hong Kong/Istanbul as the lowest-rated

3️⃣ Reviews


Review Frequency funnel chart showing how many times guests typically review (98.8% of customers review 3 times or fewer), with an outlier flagged for data-quality investigation (one listing with 283 reviews)
Seasonality streamgraph showing monthly review share by city — revealing Paris/Rome peak in European summer (Apr–Aug) while New York peaks during the November–December holiday season
Trust & Verification visual breaking down hosts by profile picture + identity verification status, showing over two-thirds of hosts are fully verified

💡 Key Insights

Top market:	Paris leads in listings & reviews; hotel rooms cost 2× Airbnb rates
Best-rated cities :	Mexico City & Rio de Janeiro (avg. 94.6–94.8 / 100)
Lowest-rated cities :	Hong Kong & Istanbul — cleanliness & value-for-money score lowest
Guest behaviour	: 96.6% of guests leave only 1 review ever
Peak season	: European summer (Apr–Aug) dominates review volume
Host trust : 66.9% of hosts are identity-verified with profile picture
COVID-19 impact :	Growth halted in 2019; new listings dropped sharply through 2020–21
